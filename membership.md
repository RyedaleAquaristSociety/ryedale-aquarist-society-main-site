So you want to become a member of Ryedale Aquarist Society? Thanks for considering joining our little club, it only costs £6 for the entire year!
It's also very easy to join - just come and see us at one of our monthly meetings and fill in our membership form (don't forget to bring £6!)

You can also arrange a meeting with our secretary by sending an email to mailto:info@RyedaleAquaristSociety.org.uk
Our secretary can arrange a phone call or face to face meetup to discuss your membership.

## Membership Benefits
Members of our club benefit from the following:

- Free entry into our monthly meetings
- Access to our members' only Facebook messenger chat
- Annual Christmas party with buffet
