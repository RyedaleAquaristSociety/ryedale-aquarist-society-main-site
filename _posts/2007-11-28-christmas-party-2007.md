*Text and photographs by David Marshall*

On Wednesday 28th November our members and local 'friends', a total of 24 people, came together in order to enjoy an early Christmas party hosted by Sue and David Marshall.

<!--more-->

Our 'good friends' Arthur & Sue Kenworthy of the Otley Aquarist Society arrived early to help with the preparations. To go with plenty of good company and food Mrs. Kenworthy put together a lovely Christmas quiz in which computer graphics partly covered the faces of well-known people from the Y.A.A.S. circuit. Well done to Mr. Steve Jones and Mr. Mick Price who took home a prize apiece for getting most of the faces correct

A big THANK YOU to everyone present for their wonderful company and for all the food and raffle prizes brought along. I don't think anyone present would forgive me if I did not pass on a special THANK YOU to the two Sue's who worked so hard in order for us all to have a night to remember.
