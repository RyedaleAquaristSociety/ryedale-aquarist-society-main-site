Since 1990 we have held our monthly meeting, or more loosely we arrange 12 meetings a year, at the Bay Horse Hotel, Market Place, Pickering and meet in the main function room (from the main entrance go up the stairs to your right). Our meetings begin at 7.30p.m. All aquarists', even those with just a Goldfish in a bowl, are welcome to attend.

<!--more-->

Remaining meeting dates for 2002 are as follows:-

- Tuesday July 23rd - Social evening with the members of Scarborough A.S.
- Wednesday September 11th - Talk on water quality by Mr. Arthur Frisby
- Wednesday October 2nd - Talk on Betta species by well known Anabantoid photographer Mr. Kevin Webb
- Wednesday November 13th - Membership discussion
- Wednesday December 11th - Christmas party

The dates for the next few meetings in 2003 are as follows:-

- Tuesday 7th January - AGM and annual trophy competition
- Wednesday 13th February
- Wednesday 5th March
- Wednesday 2nd April

New faces are always made welcome at our meetings and if you are an aquarist visiting our area on 'meeting night' please drop in and say 'hello'.

Don't forget our Open Show on Sunday 11th May, 2003.
