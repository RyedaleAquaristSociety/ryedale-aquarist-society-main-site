Since 1990 we have held our monthly meeting, or more loosely we arrange 12 meetings a year, at the Bay Horse Hotel, Market Place, Pickering and meet in the main function room (from the main entrance go up the stairs to your right). Our meetings begin at 7.30p.m. All aquarists', even those with just a Goldfish in a bowl, are welcome to attend.

<!--more-->

The dates for the next few meetings are as follows:-

- Wednesday 11th February
- Wednesday 3rd March - Our guest speaker, Mrs. Jackie Goulder, will talk about the new aquarium complex at Flamingo Land.
- Wednesday 7th April - Our Chairman, Mr. Mark Stevens, will present an illustrated talk on the evolution of the Shark.

A quick reminder to always check meeting details with David should any of the meeting events have to be changed at short notice.

New faces are always made welcome at our meetings and if you are an aquarist visiting our area on 'meeting night' please drop in and say 'hello'.

Don't forget our Open Show on Sunday 9th May, 2004.
