Did you know that Flagfish are excellent fish to keep? Please keep more Flagfish.

<!--more-->

More stuff here but there's nothing really here. I'll have to ramble a bit more about Flagfish. Did you know that Flagfish can take temperatures right down to 15c? But they are most comfortable at a minimum of around 18c.

I wouldn't keep Flagfish for algae eating. Yes they do love hair algae, but they aren't exactly prolific feeders of it, and if you have hair algae it's likely you'll still have it even with Flagfish in the tank. Flagfish seem to prefer duckweed to hair algae but they won't get rid of that either, only munch on it lightly.
