Ryedale Aquarist Society are pleased to announce the launch of our brand new website. With this we hope to provide you with the club's latest news, articles about fishkeeping, photos from our events and other interesting content written by members and guests alike.
We've used a selection of the colours from our official logo to design this site. We opted for a dark theme as many people prefer them for being "easy on the eyes".

<!--more-->

Here is a little more detail about the website technology:

It is compatible with all devices, from laptops to mobile phones, tablets to PCs. We have also built this site to be accessible, following WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines. This is extremely important to us; an accessible website is one that everyone can use, whatever their abilities, just like buildings with ramps, handrails and clear signs. It means that people with poor eyesight, colour-blindness, people who are unable to use a mouse or those using a screen reader can still read everything on the site.

We have secured ryedaleaquaristsociety.org.uk as our new domain and our site is hosted at [https://github.com](https://github.com/RyedaleAquaristSociety/ryedale-aquarist-society-main-site). The code itself is stored in a public repository which means that everyone will have access to it, and therefore the site can be recovered by anyone in an emergency.
