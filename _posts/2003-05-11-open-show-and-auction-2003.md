Ryedale Aquarist Society full Open Show and Auction of aquatic livestock and related aquatic items, on Sunday 11th May 2003 at Askham Bryan College Pickering Centre, Swainsea Lane, Pickering, N. Yorks.

<!--more-->

## Open Show

Benching of Open Show entries from 10.00a.m. to 1.00p.m.

There are 43 classes, including special classes for Catfish, Cichlids, Viviparous fish and Goldfish. Although young aquarists' are welcome, and encouraged, to show their favourite fish in any appropriate class we do provide a special junior class for aquarists' under the age of 16. To add to the aquatic flavour of our show we will be holding a special class for Amphibians.

Y.A.A.S. standards and rules etc. apply.

## Auction

Booking of auction lots on a first come, first sold basis from 10.00a.m. to 12.45p.m.

15% commission on all lots to R.A.S.

## Visiting

Experienced aquarists' will be on hand to answer any questions you may have about the aquarium hobby.

Members of the public are welcome to attend. Non exhibitors 20p, children free.

Refreshments will be available at the venue. There is plenty of free car parking both at and around the venue.
