Annual Trophy Competitions

Senior Aquarist of the Year (Tableshow points) - Mr. David Marshall

Junior Aquarist of the Year (Tableshow points) - No award was made this year.

Our members brought along a variety of fish species in order to compete, in 'friendly competition', for the four remaining trophies. At the end of the night

<!--more-->

Mr. Malcolm Short had 'swept the board' taking the British Telecom Xmas Trophy (Opaline gourami), Fishkeeping Contest (Guppy), Best Egglaying Fish (Ruby-finned shark) and Best Livebearing Fish (German yellow-tail tuxedo guppy). Well-done Malcolm.

Our monthly meetings for 2008 will continue to be held at the Bay Horse Hotel, Pickering.
