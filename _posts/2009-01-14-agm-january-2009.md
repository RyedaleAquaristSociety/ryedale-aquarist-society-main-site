*Photographs by Sue Marshall*

The AGM of the Ryedale Aquarist Society was held on Wednesday 14th January at the Bay Horse Hotel, Pickering

Chairman & Show Secretary - Mr. M. Short

Secretary & Treasurer - Mr. D. Marshall

Committee Members- Miss S. Marshall, Mrs. A. Grayson, Mrs. J. Goulder,

Mr. H. Hallsworth, Mr. F. Tolomeo.

The winners of the Annual Trophy Competitions were as follows:-

Senior Aquarist of the Year (Tableshow points) - Mr. D. Marshall

British Telecom Xmas Trophy & Owner of Best Egglaying - Mr. F. Tolomeo

Fishkeeping Contest & Best Livebearing fish - Mr. M. Short.

Our thanks to Aquarian Fish Foods who, through Dr. David Ford, sponsored the prizes for the above competitions.

Monthly meetings (1st Wednesday when possible) will be held at the Bay Horse.

The annual Open Show will be held on Sunday 26th April at Kirkbymoorside Memorial Hall. Along with 45 Classes, covering the full range of tropical and coldwater fish, we will be hosting a leg of the British Livebearer Association Fancy Guppy League.
