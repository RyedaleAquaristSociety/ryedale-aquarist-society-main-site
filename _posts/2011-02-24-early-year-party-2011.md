*Report and photographs by David Marshall*

When the adverse weather of December 2010 forced the cancellation of our annual Christmas Party the members decided that we would organise an early year party instead.

<!--more-->

Thankfully, the weather remained calm so on the evening of Thursday 24th February 2011 a total of 25 members and friends of Ryedale Aquarist Society made their way to the Mill Suite at Pickering Memorial Hall.

P2, P8 and P9 - Friends old and new were enjoying the evening.

P7 - Partygoers concentrate on Sue's quiz.

The evening began with the presentation of a 'Places quiz' that was devised by Miss Sue Marshall. The 25 questions had a mixed theme but all led to places. The joint winners were Pauline Barker's team and Steve Jones's team

For the 'pot luck supper' the people present filled a couple of large tables to the brim with a variety of sweet and savoury foods.

P11 - Mark Lyons helps people check their raffle tickets while Malcolm Short wins a prize.

P10 and P16 - Frank Tolomeo with the 'Play your cards right' board.

As you will see from the accompanying photographs Mr. Frank Tolomeo's 'Play your cards right Ryedale style' went down a storm and finished a wonderful evening in great style. Well done to David Armitage and Steve Jones who, with a little luck, played the game to perfection to take home the first prize.

P12 and P14 - Henry Hallsworth helps Frank by taking around cards for contestants to cut.

P15 - Smiles all around the room as 'Play your cards right' gets into full swing.

Finally, thank you to everyone present for their company, food and raffle prize donations. A wonderful start to a new fishkeeping year.
