*Text and photographs by David Marshall*

On Thursday 26th November our annual Christmas party was held in the Mill Suite at Pickering Memorial Hall.

A total of 27 members and friends would be in attendance and we certainly had a wonderful night.

The evening began with a special Christmas quiz that was compiled by Miss Sue Marshall. The questions were a mixed lot centered around carols, pantomimes, popular music, and sport and, of course, fish. Well done to Mrs. Pauline Barker for getting the greatest number of correct answers. As you will see from the photograph Pauline was thrilled to receive the winners prize of a marvelous Rena filter unit, presented by senior Aquarian consultant Dr. David Ford.

<!--more-->

Next came the 'pot luck supper' for which the people present brought along a rich variety of sweet and savoury items that filled the table. Thank you as we certainly had a supper to remember and here was another chance to savour Avril Bank's speciality corned beef and potato pie.

Next, with the aid of projection equipment, Mr. Frank Tolomeo presented a unique aquatic quiz in which everyday aquatic objects were photographed from strange angles. Frank, who certainly had much patience in putting his presentation together, had his audience both amused and astounded.

Finally, it was time just to relax and talk to 'old friends' before the evening closed with the drawing of the raffle. Thank you to everyone who brought along prizes and to Aquarian fish foods for their generous donation which would see every purchaser of a raffle ticket going home with a tub of fish food and a small bottle of API Melafix
